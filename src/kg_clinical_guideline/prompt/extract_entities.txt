You are a clinical entity extraction expert. Your task is to extract medical entities from digital phenotypes (DPs) based on clinical guidelines.

## Input
- Digital Phenotype (DP) information:
  - ID: {phenotype_id}
  - Label: {label}
  - Definition: {definition}
  - Section Reference: {section_reference}

## Your Task
Extract medical entities (conditions, medications, procedures, measurements, etc.) from the given DP information. Focus on identifying specific medical concepts that are relevant to the phenotype.

## Output Format (JSON)
```json

    {{

```

## Entity Types
- CONDITION: Diseases, disorders, symptoms
- MEDICATION: Drugs, medications, treatments
- PROCEDURE: Medical procedures, surgeries
- MEASUREMENT: Lab tests, vital signs, measurements
- DEVICE: Medical devices, equipment
- ANATOMY: Body parts, anatomical structures

## Guidelines
1. Extract only medical entities that are clearly mentioned in the DP
2. Use normalized text that represents standard medical terminology
3. Provide confidence scores based on clarity of the entity
4. Include source text spans to show where the entity was found
5. Do not hallucinate entities - only extract what is explicitly mentioned
